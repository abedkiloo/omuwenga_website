Zipper Sizes Explained: No. 5 vs No. 7 vs No. 10

Zipper numbers broadly indicate chain width and strength. The fabric, opening length and frequency of use should guide your choice.

No. 5 for everyday cushion covers

A common option for removable sofa cushions, pillows and lighter cover applications where a neat, flexible finish matters.

No. 7 and No. 8 for more strength

These middle sizes suit heavier covers or frequently opened sections while remaining less bulky than many No. 10 applications.

No. 10 for heavy-duty work

Often considered for thick covers, mattresses, bags and demanding commercial upholstery. Confirm that the slider matches the chain size.

Colour and slider compatibility

Match the chain colour to the fabric where it may be visible. Never force a slider intended for another size, as this can damage the chain.

Bring a sample: A short section of the existing chain or slider is the safest way to identify a replacement.

Unsure which zipper fits?
