Buying in Bulk from Gikomba: A Guide for Furniture Makers

A prepared buying list makes quotes clearer and gets materials into the workshop faster.

Write exact sizes and quantities

List the product, colour, measurement, unit and quantity. Include clear photos or samples for unusual stands, sliders or decorative accessories.

Share the complete list

Volume arrangements vary by product. Sending the full mixed-category list helps the supplier understand the total requirement before confirming.

Confirm alternatives

Decide in advance whether similar colours, finishes or sizes are acceptable if the first option is unavailable.

Review dispatch details

Confirm payment instructions, parcel size, courier charge, destination and receiving contact before funds are sent and goods dispatched.

Useful format: “Item — size/colour — quantity — acceptable alternative.” Add a photo where possible.
