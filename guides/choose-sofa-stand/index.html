Sofa stand guide

How to Choose the Right Sofa Stand for Your Furniture

The right stand affects comfort, proportion, floor clearance, stability and the final appearance of a sofa.

Start with the finished seat height

Measure the frame, cushion and intended seat height together. A taller stand can improve clearance, but too much height may make the piece uncomfortable or unstable.

Check the mounting method

Compare the new stand's plate, screw or bolt with the sofa frame. When replacing an existing leg, take a clear underside photo and record the hole spacing.

Match strength to the furniture

Large sofas and commercial seating need stronger, wider supports. Consider how weight will be distributed and how many stands the frame requires.

Choose a suitable style

Carrot, trophy, flower, Y-stand, round and spiral designs create different looks. Match the finish to nearby furniture hardware and the intended room.

Before visiting: Bring one old stand, a mounting photo, required height and quantity. This makes matching much faster.
