A Beginner's Guide to Sofa Upholstery Materials

A durable sofa depends on hidden support and finishing materials as much as the visible fabric.

Webbing creates support

Webbing or straps span the frame beneath foam and cushions. Suitable tension and spacing help distribute weight across the seat and back.

Piping defines the shape

PVC lip and piping form clean lines around cushions and panels, protect seams and create a professional finished edge.

Fasteners make covers practical

Zippers and hook-and-loop material help make covers and panels removable for cleaning, adjustment or future repair.

Hardware adds function

Stands, handles, castors, rings and cup holders affect furniture height, movement, use and appearance. Plan them before completing the upholstery.

Plan the full build: Listing all supporting and finishing items early reduces workshop delays.
