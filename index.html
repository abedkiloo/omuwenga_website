How to get what you need

Four steps.

  1. Send or bring the piece. A photo, a sample, or the old fitting is enough to start.
  2. The size and type are checked. Zipper number, stand shape, tape width or handle length.
  3. Availability is confirmed. The shop says what is in stock and the price before you pay.
  4. Collect or send. Collect in Gikomba, or ask for a Nairobi rider or a courier across Kenya.
