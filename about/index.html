OUR STORY

Built on Experience. Driven by Excellence.

Founded in 2022 and rooted in Gikomba Market, Nairobi, OMUWENGA was established with a clear understanding of the furniture trade and the people who make it thrive.

Over the years, we have grown into a specialist supplier of furniture fittings, upholstery accessories and essential components serving furniture makers, upholsterers, manufacturers, retailers and resellers.

Our journey has been shaped by the needs of the trade: the importance of having the right component, the value of dependable supply and the relationships that keep businesses moving.

Today, our ambition extends beyond supplying products. We are building a business founded on trust, thoughtful sourcing and a deep commitment to the people we serve.

With our roots firmly established in Kenya, we look towards a future of greater connection, opportunity and service across the regional furniture industry.

Our heritage informs who we are. Excellence defines how we serve. Progress shapes where we are going.
