Our story · Gikomba

Humble beginnings. The go-to fittings partner.

This is where it started — a real stall in Gikomba Market, stocked for furniture makers. Wholesale and retail, still here Monday to Saturday.

We did not begin with a showroom. We began where furniture makers already buy — in Gikomba — matching one sample, one photo, one old fitting at a time.

Humble beginnings

A stall built for the trade.

Omuwenga started as a fittings shop among the workshops of Gikomba Market, Nairobi. The counter is where sizes are checked, quotes are given, and M-Pesa, bank transfer or cash settles the order — after the price is confirmed.

That habit — listen first, match carefully, quote clearly — is still how every order is handled today, whether you collect at the stall or send for a rider or courier.

Inside the stall

Real shelves, real stock — sofa stands, piping, webbing and finishing hardware ready for the workshop.

The journey

From one stall to the go-to point.

  1. Begin
    A fittings stall in Gikomba Among furniture makers and upholsterers, Omuwenga stocked the small parts that finish a piece — zippers, stands, tape, webbing and hardware — for anyone who walked in with a sample or a size.
  2. Grow
    Trusted by workshops & resellers Word travelled with the trade. Manufacturers, fundis, upholsterers and resellers across East Africa came for trade sizes in stock, clear wholesale and retail quotes, and fittings matched to the job.
  3. Today
    Kenya’s go-to supply partner Omuwenga is the place makers turn to for furniture and upholstery fittings — still in Gikomba, Monday to Saturday, 8:00am–6:00pm, with Nairobi riders and couriers across Kenya on request.

One place for the fittings that finish every piece — the go-to supplier for zippers, sofa stands, piping, webbing, hook and loop, handles, castors and finishing hardware.

What is stocked

Six groups of fittings, matched to the piece you bring or the size you send.

Zippers & sliders

No. 5, No. 7, No. 8 and No. 10 nylon chains and matching sliders.

Sofa stands

Carrot, trophy, flower, Y-stand, round and spiral shapes.

Piping & lipping

Plastic lipping and PVC in the widths named on the shop list.

Hook & loop

25 mm and 50 mm, by the metre or by the roll.

Webbing

50 mm webbing for seats and backs.

Handles & castors

Handles, carpet castors, sofa buttons and sofa rim.
