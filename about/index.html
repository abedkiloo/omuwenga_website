About Omuwenga Suppliers

Omuwenga Suppliers is a furniture and upholstery fittings shop in Gikomba Market, Nairobi. The hours are Monday to Saturday, 8:00am–6:00pm.

What is stocked

Zippers and sliders, sofa stands and legs, piping and lipping, webbing, hook and loop, handles, castors and finishing hardware. Wholesale and retail. Payment is M-Pesa, bank transfer or cash, confirmed before you pay.

How we work

Bring a sample, a photo or the old fitting. The shop matches the size and type, confirms what is in stock, and quotes before you pay. Collect in Gikomba, or ask about a Nairobi rider or a courier across Kenya.
