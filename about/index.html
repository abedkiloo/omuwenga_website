Gikomba · Nairobi

The furniture maker’s supply partner.

Omuwenga Suppliers stocks the fittings workshops finish with — wholesale and retail from Gikomba Market, Monday to Saturday, 8:00am–6:00pm.

From a sample, a photo or the old piece, the shop matches the size and type, confirms what is in stock, and quotes clearly before you pay.

Who we serve

Built for trade, open to every buyer.

Workshops, manufacturers, upholsterers and resellers across East Africa come for zippers, sofa stands, piping, webbing, hook and loop, handles, castors and finishing hardware.

Payment is M-Pesa, bank transfer or cash. Collect in Gikomba, or ask about a Nairobi rider or a courier across Kenya.

Location and delivery · Catalogue

What is stocked

Six groups of fittings, matched to the piece you bring or the size you send.

Zippers & sliders

No. 5, No. 7, No. 8 and No. 10 nylon chains and matching sliders.

Sofa stands

Carrot, trophy, flower, Y-stand, round and spiral shapes.

Piping & lipping

Plastic lipping and PVC in the widths named on the shop list.

Hook & loop

25 mm and 50 mm, by the metre or by the roll.

Webbing

50 mm webbing for seats and backs.

Handles & castors

Handles, carpet castors, sofa buttons and sofa rim.

How we work

  1. Send or bring the pieceA photo, a sample or the old fitting is enough to start.
  2. Size and type are checkedZipper number, stand shape, tape width or handle length.
  3. Stock and price confirmedThe shop says what is available before you pay.
  4. Collect or sendGikomba collection, Nairobi rider or courier across Kenya.
