Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before you visit or order.

For product matching, send a photo, measurement and expected quantity on WhatsApp.

Which zipper size should I use for a sofa?

No. 5 works for many removable cushion covers. No. 8 or No. 10 may suit heavier covers and frequent commercial use. Slider and chain sizes must match.

How are sofa stands measured?

Measure from the mounting surface to the floor. Also confirm the mounting plate or screw type, available width and the load the furniture will carry.

Do you sell Velcro and webbing by the metre?

Common hook-and-loop and webbing options may be supplied by the metre, with roll or bulk quantities available on request.

Do you show prices and stock online?

No. This website is informational. Contact us directly for current sizes, colours, availability and pricing; sales are handled through our existing business systems.

How do I place an order?

Send your list, quantities and reference photos by WhatsApp or call us. We confirm available options, payment and pickup or delivery details directly.

Which payment methods are accepted?

M-Pesa, bank transfer and cash for shop pickup may be available. Always confirm the correct payment details with the business before sending funds.

Can you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. Local rider and countrywide parcel or courier delivery can be arranged. Charges and lead times depend on destination and parcel size.
