Frequently asked questions

Answers for the furniture trade.

For product matching, send a photo, measurement and expected quantity on WhatsApp.

Which zipper size should I use for a sofa?

No. 5 suits most removable cushion covers. No. 8 or No. 10 is better for heavier covers and frequent commercial use. Slider and chain sizes must match.

How are sofa stands measured?

Measure from the mounting surface to the floor. Also confirm the mounting plate or screw type, the available width and the load the furniture will carry.

Do you sell Velcro and webbing by the metre?

Common hook-and-loop and webbing widths are sold by the metre, with full rolls for workshop and reseller orders.

Can I see your products online?

Yes. Our live catalogue shows products and photos straight from our shop system. Trade prices depend on quantity, so we confirm the price for your order on WhatsApp or by phone.

Do you supply manufacturers and resellers?

Yes. We supply production-run quantities to manufacturers and cartons, rolls and mixed-category orders to resellers and distributors, with volume pricing. See trade & wholesale.

How do I place an order?

Send your list, quantities and reference photos by WhatsApp or call us. We confirm availability, your price, payment and pickup or delivery before you pay.

Which payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa and bank transfer, plus cash for shop pickup. Always confirm payment details directly with Omuwenga before sending funds.

Can you deliver outside Nairobi?

Yes. We arrange rider delivery within Nairobi and courier dispatch to towns across Kenya. Charges and lead times depend on destination and parcel size.

Do you supply customers outside Kenya?

We currently deliver within Kenya. Buyers from neighbouring countries can collect in Nairobi or send their own transporter; contact us to arrange.

Still unsure what fits?

Send a clear photo and measurements and we’ll match it for you.
