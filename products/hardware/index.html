Handles and Castors

The shop list includes handles marked 96–150 mm, carpet castors, sofa buttons and sofa rim. The catalogue shows handles at 5.5 inches, castors in silver and gold, buttons at 25, 28 and 30 mm, and rim in silver and gold.

What is stocked

  • Handles 96–150 mm
  • Carpet castors
  • Sofa buttons, 25, 28 and 30 mm in the catalogue
  • Sofa rim, silver and gold in the catalogue

Sizes and types

Handle length, button diameter and finish are the details printed in the catalogue and on the product names.
