Zippers and Sliders

Omuwenga stocks nylon zippers and the sliders that run on them. The sizes named in the shop list are No. 5, No. 7, No. 8 and No. 10.

What is stocked

  • No. 5 zipper
  • No. 7 zipper
  • No. 8 sliders
  • No. 10 zipper
  • No. 10 sliders
  • No. 5 sliders

Sizes and types

Zipper numbers are a general way to talk about chain size. A slider has to match the chain. The guide explains how No. 5, No. 7, No. 8 and No. 10 are usually used, and the shop confirms the piece you need.

Matched to the job

Send a photo of the chain and the slider, or bring the old piece to Gikomba. That is how the size is checked.

Available in quantity

You can ask for one piece or a larger quantity. The price is confirmed before you pay.
