Piping and Lipping

The shop list includes plastic lipping and PVC in widths such as 3/4 inch, 1 inch and 2 inch. Colours named there include gold.

What is stocked

  • Plastic lipping
  • PVC, including 1 inch PVC gold
  • 2 inch PVC

Sizes and types

Lipping and PVC are sold by the type printed on the product name. Bring a sample if you need a match.

Matched to the job

A short offcut, or a photo against a ruler, shows the width.

Available in quantity

Ask for the length or the number of rolls. The shop confirms the price.
